Frequently asked questions

Before you book

What is the minimum booking?

Hourly cleaning has a 3-hour minimum per cleaner. First-time bookings get 20% off the standard hourly rate; recurring plans are billed at AED 50 per cleaner-hour.

Can you provide cleaning materials?

Yes. Ordinary supplies and basic equipment are included in the price, along with local transport within the agreed service area. Any exceptional materials or charges are confirmed with you before booking.

How are villa and larger-space prices confirmed?

Villa pricing depends on size and layout. Custom villa and larger-space quotes can be confirmed directly through WhatsApp.

Are there hidden call-out fees?

No hidden call-out fees are added to the published service rates.

How do I get started?

Complete the free quote form or message the Oasis Safa Clean team on WhatsApp with your city, property size and preferred date.
